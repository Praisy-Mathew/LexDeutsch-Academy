LEVEL A2 Duration: 2 Months

A2 — Elementary German

Expand your communication skills and gain confidence in everyday situations.

The course builds on your A1 foundation and helps you communicate more comfortably in daily situations.

Key Areas Covered:

  • Routine everyday interactions & social situations
  • Talking about past events, family, and hobbies
  • Shopping, directions, dining, and appointments
  • Expanded grammar structures and practical vocabulary

LEVEL B1 Duration: 3 Months

B1 — Intermediate German

Develop independent language skills for:

  • Work
  • Study
  • Everyday life
  • German-speaking environments

The course focuses on developing stronger communication and practical language skills. Essential milestone for German vocational training (Ausbildung) and citizenship/residency requirements.

Independent User (CEFR B1) Prerequisite: A2 completed

B2 — Advanced German

  • Professional careers
  • Visa requirements

Build greater confidence and communication ability for academic and professional environments. Vital qualification for nursing licensure, engineering positions, and university admissions across Germany.
