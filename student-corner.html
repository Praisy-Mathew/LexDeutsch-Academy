Code of Conduct

Classroom Rules

A positive classroom environment helps everyone learn better. We encourage our students to adhere to these core principles:

Speak German as Much as Possible

Immerse yourself daily and use newly acquired phrases in class.

Punctual & Regular Attendance

Be punctual and attend every session to maintain continuous momentum.

Timely Assignments & Homework

Complete assignments and homework on time to solidify classroom lessons.

Respect Teachers & Classmates

Foster a welcoming, supportive, and motivating atmosphere for everyone.

Mobile Phones on Silent

Keep phones on silent mode during classes to preserve focused attention.

Active Conversation Practice

Participate enthusiastically in speaking drills, role-plays, and group talk.

Always Ask Questions: Actively engage in learning—there are no wrong questions in mastering German!

Open to All

Who Can Join?

German learning is open to everyone. No previous knowledge is required for the A1 course.

Students

Aspiring university entrants seeking bachelor's, master's, or PhD programs in Germany.

Nurses & Healthcare Professionals

Registered nurses and medical staff preparing for clinical practice licensing in Germany.

Working Professionals

Engineers, IT specialists, and managers advancing their global career prospects.

Job Seekers

Skilled individuals preparing for the German Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte).

Migrants & Family Reunion

Spouses and families fulfilling official A1 / B1 Goethe embassy requirements.

Anyone Interested in German

Enthusiasts who love the German language, European culture, and personal growth.

Official Certifications

Exam Preparation

Prepare with focused support for internationally recognised German language examinations. International certification is awarded by the respective examination bodies, such as TELC or Goethe, after successfully passing their examinations.

TELC Preparation

Develop the specific language skills, pattern familiarity, and exam techniques required for TELC examinations.

Goethe Preparation

Prepare for Goethe examinations with structured mock evaluations, past papers, and intensive speaking interview practice.
