Political System

Government

Germany is a stable, democratic constitutional state grounded in the Basic Law (Grundgesetz).

  • Type: Federal Parliamentary Republic
  • Head of State: Federal President (Bundespräsident)
  • Head of Government: Federal Chancellor (Bundeskanzler)
  • Parliament: Bundestag and Bundesrat
